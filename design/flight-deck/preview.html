00 — Orientation

Flight Deck

A proposed design system for Adil Mahroof's portfolio. The organising idea is an instrument panel reading out an engineering career — dense, precise, tabular, with one amber signal colour used only where something is worth signalling.

The rule that carries the identity: data is monospace, prose is sans, never the reverse. Dates, metrics, statuses, tags and indices are mono and tabular. Sentences are Inter. Almost everything else follows.

Every word, number and date below comes from the existing content files. Nothing has been rewritten or invented. Three things are derived from content rather than copied verbatim, and each is flagged in place: project years (read off the presentation venues), project status, and the four hero readouts. See SPEC.md §8.
Dark is the primary theme. Light is designed as a real counterpart — printed telemetry on paper — not a derived inversion. Toggle it top-right and check both; the accent hue deliberately changes between them, because #ff9e1b on white is roughly 1.9:1 and unusable.
